Agent review

When SkillHub's agent reviews a skill, it adds:

  • A TLDR: what the skill does, what you end up with, when to use it and how it differs from a close neighbour.
  • Tags: what area it's for, how it works, what it needs and what it can change on its own. The Tags page explains each one.
  • A note when there's something to know before installing, such as an account it needs, what it sends to a third party, or a safety check it switches off.
  • A bundle when it's meant to be used with other skills of its repo: see Bundles.

Bundles

A bundle is skills of one repo that are meant to be installed and used together, such as the steps of a workflow: one writes a spec, the next turns it into tickets, another implements them. A repo that only collects unrelated skills isn't a bundle. SkillHub has 37.

  • In the list, a bundle has a row of its own that names its skills in the order they're used. A skill worth installing alone also keeps a row of its own, which says what bundle it's part of. One that only works with the rest, such as one that works on another's output, is only listed inside its bundle, unless the list is set to show Skills, which lists every skill on its own. Set to show Bundles, it lists only bundles.
  • Its page explains how its skills work together, what each one needs from the others, and how to install them all at once.
  • Its numbers come from its skills: Published is when its oldest skill was first seen, Total and Weekly Downloads are its most downloaded skill's, and Stars are its repo's. The filters' sliders use these numbers, so an old bundle with a few new skills isn't in "Published: last 90 days".
  • Tags and the search match a bundle through any of its skills, and grey out its other skills.

Up-to-date

SkillHub follows each listed skill's SKILL.md in its repo. A skill's page says when its SKILL.md has changed since its TLDR was written.
