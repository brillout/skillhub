How skills get here

SkillHub lists 1,287 agent skills, out of the 3,434 it reviewed. Each was read and judged on whether people looking for skills would find it useful.

1. Finding skills

Skills come from skills.sh, with their installs, and their repo's stars from GitHub. A skill is reviewed once it has 5,000 installs and its repo 100 stars. 906 skills are under that line, and none is waiting for review. Numbers are as of Nov 7, 2026.

2. Reviewing them

A review reads the skill's whole SKILL.md, not only its description. 1,876 skills were turned down:

  • 1,384 tool docs: manuals for one product, framework, cloud or language, such as a cloud's CLI or a framework's best practices. They only matter once you've chosen the product. A skill that gets a job done through a tool, such as testing, scraping or generating media, isn't tool docs.
  • 249 not in English.
  • 109 copies: a skill republished under another name, or listed twice, such as under its repo's old name.
  • 55 retired: its SKILL.md points to a replacement.
  • 79 for other reasons: it only works inside one company's codebase or brand, does nothing for whoever installs it, or does something you wouldn't want, such as installing dozens of other skills without asking or removing the watermarks that mark images as AI-generated.

Another 240 skills are off the list because their SKILL.md is gone from their repo, and 31 because they aren't on GitHub.

3. Describing the rest

Each listed skill gets:

  • A TLDR: what the skill does, what you end up with, when to use it and how it differs from a close neighbour.
  • Tags: what area it's for, how it works, what it needs and what it can change on its own. The Tags page explains each one.
  • A note when there's something to know before installing, such as an account it needs, what it sends to a third party, or a safety check it switches off.
  • A bundle when skills of one repo are meant to be used together, such as the steps of a workflow. SkillHub has 43.

4. Keeping it current

SkillHub follows each listed skill's SKILL.md in its repo. A skill's page says when its SKILL.md has changed since its TLDR was written, and a skill whose SKILL.md is gone comes off the list.

What to keep in mind

Reviews can be wrong, and no one runs the skills to test them: a review reads the SKILL.md and judges it. Install counts come from skills.sh and stars from GitHub. Something wrong or missing? Open an issue.
