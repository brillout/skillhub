TLDR

Firecrawl Workflows turns Firecrawl, a web search, scraping and browser service, into complete jobs. firecrawl-workflows is the entry point. It matches a request to one workflow, or runs a generic process that gathers evidence and writes it up when none fits. Most workflows follow one pattern: infer the inputs, ask at most three questions and only when blocked, collect evidence with Firecrawl, split independent parts across subagents where the agent has them, and end with sources and the inputs to rerun the job. For research there are web reports (deep-research), literature reviews over Firecrawl's paper index (research-papers), market figures and competitor tracking. Lead work runs from exporting directories (company-directories) to finding prospects through Alexandria, Firecrawl's catalog of data providers (lead-gen), to enriching people and companies you already have (lead-research). Other workflows audit a site's SEO, test a live site, walk through a product's flows, extract a site's design system and read dashboard metrics. knowledge-base saves web docs as local files, and knowledge-ingest does the same for portals that need a browser. shop compares products and adds one to a cart only when asked. Some skills hand off to others: deep-research passes literature to research-papers.
