TLDR

Academic Research Skills (ARS), by Cheng-I Wu, treats the AI as a copilot: it hunts down references, formats citations and checks consistency, while you define the question, choose the method and interpret the results. deep-research frames a research question with you, searches and grades sources and writes an APA 7.0 report, or guides you through Socratic questions when the idea is still vague. Its question brief, bibliography and synthesis hand over to academic-paper, which interviews you about the paper, then outlines, drafts, cites and formats it, and later revises it from reviewer comments. In academic-paper-reviewer, five role-separated seats, including a Devil's Advocate, review the draft and a synthesizer writes a decision letter and a revision roadmap that academic-paper's revision mode takes in. academic-pipeline chains them into ten stages, with integrity checks of references, data and claims before review and again before formatting, a re-review that verifies the revision, and a closing record of the human-AI collaboration. Each stage waits for your confirmation. The skills share contracts and protocols in the repo's shared/ folder and read each other's files, so they're installed together; the plugin also adds sr-screener, a study-screening skill, plus slash commands, agents and hooks.

Its skills, in the order they're used

The ones marked “Only in the bundle” need the rest of it, so they're only listed here. The others also have their own entry in the skills list.

  1. academic-pipeline

    Only in the bundle 7.5K total downloads

    Takes a paper from research to finished manuscript in ten stages, calling sibling skills, with integrity checks and your approval between stages.

    Dispatches deep-research, academic-paper and academic-paper-reviewer for its stages and does no research, writing or reviewing itself; it also runs checker scripts from the repo's scripts/ folder, so it needs them and the whole repo, as the plugin installs it.

  2. deep-research

    Only in the bundle 8K total downloads

    Researches any academic topic, from framing the question and grading sources to a cited APA 7.0 report, with a Socratic mode for vague ideas.

    Its report compiler runs the writing-quality check in academic-paper's folder, its bibliography and three-way scan use academic-paper-reviewer's review-criteria table, and its ethics review reads registries in the repo's shared/ folder, so it needs the whole repo, as the plugin installs it.

  3. academic-paper

    Only in the bundle 11.3K total downloads

    Drafts, revises and formats academic papers through an interview-first, phase-by-phase pipeline with citation checks and a simulated peer review.

    Its full mode loads writer and evaluator contracts from the repo's shared/ folder, revision mode runs scripts from its scripts/ folder, and plan mode reads its questions from deep-research's Socratic mentor file, so it needs the whole repo, as the plugin installs it.

  4. academic-paper-reviewer

    Only in the bundle 10.2K total downloads

    Simulates a journal peer review of your paper with five reviewer roles, then writes an editorial decision letter and a revision roadmap.

    Its full and methodology-focus reviews load their contracts from the repo's shared/ folder, and its revision coaching reads its questions from deep-research's Socratic mentor file, so it needs the whole repo, as the plugin installs it.
