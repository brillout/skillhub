Use it when
you want an agent to build a mobile app or screen that holds up next to the best apps in its category
It does
appllama-usage studies the real screens and flows of top-grossing apps; appllama-app-design-skill builds Expo screens to strict native, navigation, anti-slop and motion rules
You get

Needs

TLDR

The two skills split one job: usage decides what to study, design decides how to build. With the Appllama MCP connected, appllama-usage finds a category's winning apps, walks their screens in journey order, saves notes and images under research/ and writes the category's design language to patterns.md. Its playbooks then build an app from a spec and navigation map, or rebuild one screen against a board of about 60 reference screens, handing every screen to appllama-app-design-skill. That skill sets the bar: semantic colors in both themes, native controls, navigation that can't back into a finished sign-in or purchase, one accent and one radius scale, purposeful motion and a definition of done. Both end in the same loop: run the flow in the iOS Simulator or Android emulator, record it, watch it at full speed and frame by frame, fix and repeat. The MCP is added as a custom connector at mcp.appllama.io, and each of its calls but get_credits spends one of Pro's 1,500 monthly credits.
