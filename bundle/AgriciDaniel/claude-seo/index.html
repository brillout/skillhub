TLDR

Claude SEO is a Claude Code plugin whose skills share Python tools and subagents kept outside their folders, and many read the seo skill's references, so it's meant to be installed whole. seo is the entry point: /seo <command> <url> loads the matching skill. seo-audit crawls up to 500 pages, has subagents check technical SEO, content, schema, sitemaps, speed, visuals, AI search, agent readiness, search experience and backlinks in parallel, adds local, maps, clustering, e-commerce or drift checks when they apply, and scores the site out of 100; seo-page reviews one URL. Each other skill takes one job: seo-technical, seo-sitemap, seo-images, seo-hreflang, seo-schema, seo-content for E-E-A-T (Google's experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust criteria), seo-geo for citations in AI answers, seo-sxo for whether a page is the type that ranks, seo-local, seo-maps, seo-backlinks and seo-ecommerce. seo-google brings Google's own figures. seo-plan, seo-cluster, seo-content-brief, seo-competitor-pages and seo-programmatic plan sites and pages, seo-flow supplies the author's FLOW prompts, and seo-drift snapshots a page to catch regressions. The plugin also installs seo-agentic, and seo-dataforseo and seo-image-gen, optional front ends to DataForSEO's and Gemini's paid APIs, which SkillHub doesn't list.

Its skills, in the order they're used

The ones marked “Only in the bundle” need the rest of it, so they're only listed here. The others also have their own entry in the skills list.

  1. seo

    Only in the bundle 9.9K total downloads

    Routes /seo commands to Claude SEO's sub-skills and runs full-site audits with parallel subagents, scored out of 100.

    Loads the other Claude SEO skills for each /seo command, hands audits to the plugin's subagents and runs the plugin's Python tools; alone, it only has its scoring and quality rules.

  2. seo-audit

    Only in the bundle 8.7K total downloads

    Audits a whole website for SEO with parallel subagents, then writes a scored report and a prioritized action plan.

    Hands each area to the specialist subagents that come with the Claude SEO plugin, or runs their checks inline, and renders pages and builds its PDF with the plugin's Python tools.

  3. seo-page

    Only in the bundle 7.5K total downloads

    Reviews one URL's titles, headings, content, metadata, schema and images, and returns a score card with prioritized fixes.

    Runs the plugin's metadata script to catch templated descriptions, and takes its word-count floors from the seo skill's quality-gates reference.

  4. seo-plan

    7.4K total downloads

    Writes an SEO strategy for a new or existing site: competitor analysis, site structure, content calendar and a 12-month roadmap.

  5. seo-technical

    Only in the bundle 7.9K total downloads

    Audits a site's technical SEO across nine areas, from crawlability and indexing to Core Web Vitals and JavaScript rendering.

    Runs the plugin's Python tools for its sitemap, JavaScript-rendering and agent checks, and for Google field data.

  6. seo-sitemap

    Only in the bundle 7.9K total downloads

    Validates a site's XML sitemaps against Google's limits and rules, or generates new ones from an industry template.

    Finds sitemaps with the plugin's discovery script and generates new ones from the industry templates in seo-plan's folder.

  7. seo-images

    Only in the bundle 7.4K total downloads

    Audits a page's images for alt text, size, format, dimensions and lazy loading, and converts files to WebP or AVIF with SEO metadata.

    Runs the plugin's HTML parser to tell lazy-loaders apart and its IPTC script to audit and add the AI-image label; file conversion uses exiftool, cwebp, ImageMagick or FFmpeg directly.

  8. seo-hreflang

    7.4K total downloads

    Validates and generates hreflang tags for multilingual sites, and checks content parity and cultural fit across language versions.

  9. seo-programmatic

    Only in the bundle 7.2K total downloads

    Plans and audits pages generated at scale from data, with quality gates against thin content, templated pages and index bloat.

    Runs the plugin's metadata script over the whole page set, a gate that fails the set when its titles and descriptions share one generated shape.

  10. seo-google

    Only in the bundle 7K total downloads

    Pulls Search Console performance, indexing status, Core Web Vitals field data and GA4 organic traffic from Google's APIs for SEO analysis.

    Every command runs one of the plugin's Python scripts for Google's APIs, from the credential check to its PDF report builder.

  11. seo-drift

    Only in the bundle 6.3K total downloads

    Snapshots a page's SEO elements as a baseline and diffs later states against it, to catch regressions after deploys.

    Baseline, compare and history are the plugin's drift scripts, which fetch and parse pages with its other tools and keep snapshots in SQLite.

  12. seo-content

    Only in the bundle 8.5K total downloads

    Scores a page's content for E-E-A-T, readability, thinness and AI citation readiness, and cleans AI-typical phrasing from your own drafts.

    Reads its E-E-A-T criteria and score bands from the seo skill's folder, and cleans drafts with a script that ships with the plugin.

  13. seo-content-brief

    6.5K total downloads

    Writes SEO content briefs from the top-ranking competitors: an outline with word counts per section, keyword placement, meta tags and content gaps.

  14. seo-cluster

    Only in the bundle 7.4K total downloads

    Groups keywords by how many Google results they share and designs pillar-and-spoke content clusters with an internal link plan.

    Fetches pages with the plugin's render script and checks DataForSEO costs with its cost script; its clustering method, link rules and map template are in its own folder.

  15. seo-sxo

    Only in the bundle 6.2K total downloads

    Checks whether a page is the type Google rewards for its keyword, then scores it against personas and user stories drawn from search results.

    Renders and parses the page with the plugin's render_page.py and parse_html.py, which its quality checklist requires; its taxonomy, persona and wireframe references are in its own folder.

  16. seo-flow

    Only in the bundle 6K total downloads

    Applies prompts from the FLOW SEO framework to your URL or topic, one stage at a time: find, leverage, optimize, win or local.

    Runs the plugin's sync script for /seo flow sync, the command that refreshes its prompts; the 41 prompts themselves are in its own folder.

  17. seo-competitor-pages

    8.3K total downloads

    Outlines "X vs Y", "alternatives to X" and roundup pages: structure, feature table, schema markup and target keywords.

  18. seo-schema

    Only in the bundle 7.7K total downloads

    Detects, validates and generates Schema.org JSON-LD for a page, and flags structured data types Google has retired.

    Reads its full list of schema types from the seo skill's folder; its templates and extraction script ship with the plugin, not the skill.

  19. seo-geo

    Only in the bundle 7.9K total downloads

    Audits how likely AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and other answer engines are to cite your pages, and what to change.

    Runs the plugin's Google update ledger to check AI-search visibility changes against confirmed updates; its other references are in its own folder.

  20. seo-local

    Only in the bundle 7.9K total downloads

    Audits a local business site's Google Business Profile signals, reviews, NAP consistency, citations and local schema, and scores it out of 100.

    Reads its ranking-factor, local schema and citation references from the seo skill's folder.

  21. seo-maps

    Only in the bundle 6.8K total downloads

    Audits how a local business shows up on Google Maps, Bing, Apple and OpenStreetMap: geo-grid rankings, Business Profile, reviews, competitors and listing consistency.

    Reads its geo-grid method, Business Profile checklist, map API references and local schema types from the seo skill's folder.

  22. seo-backlinks

    Only in the bundle 7.8K total downloads

    Analyzes a site's backlinks, anchor text, toxic links and competitor gaps from free and paid link data, without scoring what it didn't measure.

    Pulls every data source and runs its required report validator through the plugin's Python tools, and reads toxic-link criteria from the seo skill's folder.

  23. seo-ecommerce

    Only in the bundle 6.8K total downloads

    Audits product pages and Product schema for e-commerce SEO, and with DataForSEO compares Google Shopping and Amazon prices, sellers and keyword gaps.

    Renders and parses pages, calls DataForSEO's Merchant API, checks costs and probes UCP profiles with the plugin's Python scripts.
