AI Engineering from Scratch placement and quizzes Bundle · 3 skills

Use it when
you want to start the free AI Engineering from Scratch course with your coding agent, and check yourself after each phase
It does
places you with a ten-question quiz, saves a study plan to LEARNING.md, and quizzes you with eight questions at the end of each phase
You get
an entry phase, a 20-phase path marked Skip, Review or Do with hours, and per-phase grades with the lessons to re-read
Not included
learn, the repo's tutor that teaches lesson by lesson from LEARNING.md, and course-guide, which finds the lessons on a topic; the repo's installer offers both

TLDR

The course is 542 lessons in 20 phases, from linear algebra to autonomous agents. These three skills start you on it and check you along the way; the teaching itself is the repo's learn skill, which isn't listed here. start-learning is the front door: three questions about why you're learning, your weekly hours and what you want to build, then find-your-level's placement quiz, then a LEARNING.md with your mission, entry phase, a Skip, Review or Do path with hours, a progress log and a review queue that learn keeps up to date. find-your-level also works alone and shows the same path in the conversation. Ask start-learning for only the MCP, Agent Skills or reverse engineering track and it hands you to that track's own tutor instead, also not listed. When you finish a phase, check-understanding writes eight multiple-choice questions from that phase's lesson docs, asks them one at a time, grades you from Mastered to Start Over and names the lessons to re-read. Both quizzes keep the answers hidden until you reply. Lesson docs and hour estimates come from a clone of the repo or are fetched from GitHub.
