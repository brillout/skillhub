TLDR

The core, antislop, does the work and is the one skill that works alone. Unless you've saved a default with npx antislop-ai --mode, it asks each session whether to apply its rules while building or to audit finished work. It holds every technique to a purpose test, so a gradient or badge stays only with a written reason; sets three liveliness dials, energy, rhythm and motion, so that removing slop doesn't leave a sterile page; and ends with the Delivery Gate, a four-block PASS/FAIL check. Each companion skill says to load the core with it for one kind of task, cites the core's rules by number, and adds a catalog of patterns, each with a tell, why it reads as AI and a fix, plus a checklist run alongside the gate. antislop-ui covers color, layout, decoration, dashboards, motion and performance. antislop-copywriting covers AI writing tells in headlines and prose, with before-and-after rewrites and a draft, audit and final loop. antislop-human covers contrast, keyboard, focus and affordances, with a contrast-ratio script. antislop-layoutmobile covers reflow from phone to desktop. antislop-code covers comments only, never the code.

Its skills, in the order they're used

The ones marked “Only in the bundle” need the rest of it, so they're only listed here. The others also have their own entry in the skills list.

  1. antislop

    9.3K total downloads

    Filters the generic AI look out of interfaces with 40 rules and a PASS/FAIL gate, while building or as an audit of finished work.

  2. antislop-ui

    Only in the bundle 8.2K total downloads

    Catalogs AI-default UI patterns, from purple gradients and bento grids to invented dashboard numbers, each with why it reads as generated and a fix.

    Says to load the core antislop skill with it: its fixes cite the core's rules by number and its RHYTHM and MOTION dials, and the core's Delivery Gate stays the final check.

  3. antislop-copywriting

    Only in the bundle 7.8K total downloads

    Keeps AI tells out of headlines, CTAs and prose, from buzzwords and rule-of-three padding to em dashes, without inventing facts or erasing your voice.

    Says to load the core antislop skill with it, which holds the hard bans it cites by number, such as no em dashes and no invented numbers, and the R-37 rule for asking about em dashes in your writing sample; its checklist runs alongside the core's Delivery Gate.

  4. antislop-human

    Only in the bundle 7.5K total downloads

    Keeps interfaces usable for everyone: contrast, keyboard and focus, states, zoom and real controls, with a script that computes WCAG contrast ratios.

    Says to load the core antislop skill with it: it cites the core's contrast, keyboard, state and affordance rules by number and runs its checklist alongside the core's Delivery Gate; the contrast checker is its own.

  5. antislop-layoutmobile

    Only in the bundle 7.2K total downloads

    Makes layouts reflow from phone to desktop instead of shrinking: breakpoints set by content, mobile type and spacing, collapsing grids, no overflow, thumb-sized targets.

    Says to load the core antislop skill with it: it cites the core's mobile and verification rules by number and runs its checklist alongside the core's Delivery Gate.

  6. antislop-code

    Only in the bundle 7.3K total downloads

    Cleans AI-slop comments out of code, such as banners, narration and signature echoes, keeps the ones that explain why, and never touches the code.

    Says to load the core antislop skill with it and to run its comment checklist alongside the core's Delivery Gate; its own rules cite only one core rule, R-31.
