TLDR

OpenSEO is an open-source SEO tool whose MCP server gives the agent keyword, SERP (search results page), backlink, local and Search Console data; every skill works through it. They share one context per project, stored in OpenSEO: the business, goal, positioning, writing preferences, competitors, key pages and a research log. seo-project-setup fills it in one interview; each workflow asks only for what it lacks, saves back what it learned, and reuses research from the log's last 30 days instead of spending credits again. That's how the steps hand off: keywords that keyword-research saves and tags are a starting set for keyword-clustering, and competitors saved by setup or competitive-landscape tell competitor-analysis what's known about a rival and give link-prospecting backlink profiles to mine. seo-coach is the entry point: it reads where the site stands, explains plainly and suggests the next skill. seo-audit finds the few changes most likely to grow traffic that converts; keyword-research finds terms to target and keyword-clustering maps them to pages; competitive-landscape surveys a market and competitor-analysis studies one rival; link-prospecting finds sites to ask for links and drafts the outreach. Each of these six ends in a self-contained HTML report that seo-report writes and saves.

Its skills, in the order they're used

Each also works alone, so each also has its own entry in the skills list.

  1. seo-coach

    7.1K total downloads

    Puts the agent in a friendly SEO coach mode that reads your OpenSEO project, explains numbers in plain words and suggests one next step.

  2. seo-project-setup

    7.1K total downloads

    Interviews you about a website's goals, positioning, competitors and key pages, and saves them as shared OpenSEO project context for the other skills.

  3. seo-audit

    5.6K total downloads

    Audits a website with OpenSEO's crawl and search data, then reports the one to three changes most likely to grow traffic that converts.

  4. keyword-research

    7.4K total downloads

    Turns seed topics into a prioritized keyword shortlist with volume, difficulty, intent and live search-result checks, using OpenSEO's SEO data.

  5. keyword-clustering

    7.1K total downloads

    Groups keywords into page-level clusters by search intent and assigns each to an existing page, a new page, or a later or do-not-target list.

  6. competitive-landscape

    7K total downloads

    Maps who wins search in a market, what content and backlinks work for them, and where the openings are, using OpenSEO data.

  7. competitor-analysis

    7.1K total downloads

    Studies one competitor's organic traffic, ranking keywords, content themes and backlinks with OpenSEO data, and reports where you can outrank it.

  8. link-prospecting

    7K total downloads

    Finds pages and authors likely to link to your page or asset, looks up their public contact paths, and drafts personalized outreach.
