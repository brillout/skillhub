TLDR

BMad Method carries every decision forward as files. The bmad skill's setup creates the _bmad/ folder whose scripts the other skills run, and bmad later answers what to run next. Work is grouped into initiatives, each a ticket tree: the initiative, its epics and their stories. A small change goes straight to bmad-build, which plans, implements, has a subagent review the diff and commits locally; bmad-build-auto does the same unattended. Bigger work gets planning first. bmad-brainstorming, bmad-forge-idea, bmad-deep-recon and bmad-prfaq explore and test ideas; John (bmad-agent-pm) coaches PRDs and other product documents; bmad-ux writes DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md; bmad-architecture records the decisions separately built parts must share. Sofia (bmad-agent-po), in the full install but not listed here, turns those documents into an initiative's requirements, epics and stories. Mary, Sally, Winston and Amelia are personas that stay on and run these skills from a menu. After a build, bmad-code-review, bmad-walkthrough and bmad-qa-generate-e2e-tests check it; bmad-retrospective then judges each finished epic by its own criteria. bmad-party-mode, bmad-advanced-elicitation and bmad-review bring other views to a draft or a change, bmad-project-context keeps AGENTS.md's agent rules verified, and bmad-customize changes a skill through override files. Smithy (bmad-toolsmith) builds your own skills, and bmad-eval measures them.
