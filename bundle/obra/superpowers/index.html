TLDR

Superpowers makes its skills fire on their own: using-superpowers, loaded at the start of each session, has the agent check for a matching skill before every reply. Building something then follows one path. brainstorming asks what you actually want and ends with a design you approve, in chat for small changes or as a document for projects. using-git-worktrees sets up an isolated branch with a clean test baseline, and writing-plans breaks the design into small tasks, each with exact files, code and checks. The plan runs either through subagent-driven-development, a fresh subagent per task with a review after each, or through executing-plans, inline with one review at the end. Throughout, test-driven-development makes every change start from a failing test, and finishing-a-development-branch verifies the tests, then merges, opens a PR, keeps or discards the branch. Other skills step in when needed: systematic-debugging for bugs, verification-before-completion before claiming anything works, requesting-code-review and receiving-code-review around reviews, dispatching-parallel-agents for independent tasks, and writing-skills for writing new skills test-first.
