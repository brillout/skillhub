Technical article writing Bundle · 3 skills

Use it when
you want to publish a tutorial, explainer, benchmark, bug hunt or postmortem for developers, from idea to finished post

technical-article-writer runs the interview, titles and draft, and calls copywriting-hooks for the opening and copywriting-cta for the ending

TLDR

Three skills from samber/cc-skills that work as one pipeline. technical-article-writer starts by asking the topic, a single objective, the audience, content type, length and a one-sentence thesis, then tests the idea for novelty and offers ten titles. For the opening it hands over to copywriting-hooks, which proposes 3 or 4 hooks built on different levers, such as a curiosity gap, a contrarian claim or a scene, two candidates each, and waits for your choice; the writer then adds the stakes and a promise around it. After the body and conclusion, copywriting-cta takes the objective from the interview, asks what it still needs, such as the publication context, your relationship with readers and the funnel stage, and prescribes the end-of-article CTA: exact copy, placement, any persuasion mechanism such as urgency or social proof, an A/B test and accessibility checks. A humanizer pass follows that leaves the title and hook alone, then image suggestions and a final pick among the top three titles. Both copywriting skills also work alone, for any long-form article.
