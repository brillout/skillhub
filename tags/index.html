Tags

Tags say what area a skill is for, how it works, what it needs, what it can change on its own, who publishes it and what it's about. Faceted tags carry a prefix, such as needs:cli; topics don't. Tags were assigned from each skill's SKILL.md on Nov 7, 2026. Pick a tag to list its skills.
