TLDR

A reference of Python code, not a workflow. The SKILL.md covers billing intervals, subscription states from trial through active and past due to canceled, with pause and resume, dunning through retry schedules, notifications, grace periods and account restrictions, and proration on upgrades, seat changes and billing-frequency changes. Its quick start shows the calls; the reference file holds the classes. A subscription moves through trial, activation, past due and cancellation, at once or at period end. The billing engine builds an invoice with subscription and usage line items and tax, charges the customer through Stripe, and starts dunning when the charge fails. The dunning manager retries after 3, 7 and 14 days with an email each time, then cancels the subscription. Proration credits the unused part of the old plan and charges the new one for the rest of the period; added seats are charged, removed seats aren't refunded. Tax comes from a fixed table of a few US state, EU VAT and Australian GST rates, with VAT number validation left as a stub. Invoices render to HTML, and usage billing has tiered and per-unit pricing.

Billing Automation

Master automated billing systems including recurring billing, invoice generation, dunning management, proration, and tax calculation.

When to Use This Skill

  • Implementing SaaS subscription billing
  • Automating invoice generation and delivery
  • Managing failed payment recovery (dunning)
  • Calculating prorated charges for plan changes
  • Handling sales tax, VAT, and GST
  • Processing usage-based billing
  • Managing billing cycles and renewals

Core Concepts

1. Billing Cycles

Common Intervals:

  • Monthly (most common for SaaS)
  • Annual (discounted long-term)
  • Quarterly
  • Weekly
  • Custom (usage-based, per-seat)
2. Subscription States
trial → active → past_due → canceled
              → paused → resumed
3. Dunning Management

Automated process to recover failed payments through:

  • Retry schedules
  • Customer notifications
  • Grace periods
  • Account restrictions
4. Proration

Adjusting charges when:

  • Upgrading/downgrading mid-cycle
  • Adding/removing seats
  • Changing billing frequency
