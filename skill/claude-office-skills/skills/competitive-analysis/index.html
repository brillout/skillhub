you need to compare your product with competitors for strategy, positioning or a sales team

profiles each competitor's strengths, weaknesses and strategy, compares features and pricing tiers, and places everyone on a positioning map

TLDR

From Claude Office Skills. You give your own company or product for context, the competitors to analyze (or ask the agent to identify them), the market, region and the aspects that matter, and pick a quick comparison, a full profile, a strategic analysis or a battle card. The agent works from Porter's competitor analysis (each rival's goals, current strategy, assumptions and capabilities, combined into how it's likely to respond) and a positioning matrix of price against breadth of focus. The report follows one template: market size and growth, a profile per competitor, a feature matrix rated strong, partial or absent, pricing by tier, a positioning map, your SWOT, your advantages and theirs, immediate and medium-term actions, and battle cards covering their pitch, your counter, differentiators, win themes and objection handling. It closes by reminding you that competitive intelligence goes stale and key figures need checking. Its frontmatter lists document, spreadsheet and chart tools of the repo's office-mcp server, but its instructions never call them.

Competitive Analysis Skill

Overview

I help you systematically analyze competitors to inform strategic decisions. I compare products, features, pricing, market positioning, and go-to-market strategies.

What I can do:

  • Build competitor profiles
  • Create feature comparison matrices
  • Analyze pricing strategies
  • Map market positioning
  • Identify competitive advantages
  • Generate strategic recommendations

What I cannot do:

  • Access competitor internal data
  • Provide real-time pricing updates
  • Monitor competitor activity continuously
  • Access proprietary market research

How to Use Me

Step 1: Define the Scope

Tell me:

  • Your company/product (for context)
  • Competitors to analyze (or ask me to identify them)
  • Industry/market segment
  • Geographic scope
  • Specific aspects to focus on
Step 2: Choose Analysis Type
  • Quick Comparison: Feature/pricing matrix
  • Full Profile: Comprehensive competitor deep-dive
  • Strategic Analysis: SWOT, positioning, recommendations
  • Battle Card: Sales-focused competitor summary
Step 3: Specify Output
  • Comparison tables
  • Positioning maps
  • SWOT analyses
  • Strategic recommendations
  • Sales battle cards

Tips for Better Results

  1. Provide your company context for relevant comparison
  2. Name specific competitors or describe the criteria to identify them
  3. Specify focus areas (features, pricing, positioning, etc.)
  4. Share any known information about competitors
  5. Indicate the purpose (strategic planning, sales enablement, etc.)
  6. Request battle cards for sales teams

Limitations

  • Analysis based on publicly available information
