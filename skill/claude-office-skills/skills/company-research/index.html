Use it when
you're weighing a company as an investment, partner, acquisition target or competitor and want a structured first look
It does
classifies the revenue model, places the company in its value chain, rates Porter's five forces and checks six kinds of moat, such as network effects or switching costs
You get
a Markdown report with an attractive, neutral or caution verdict, TAM, SAM and SOM, a competitor table, SWOT, leadership and governance, a financial snapshot and a risk table
Unlike
the repo's stock-analysis, it covers the business and its market rather than share-price ratios and chart patterns
Depth
a one-page overview, a standard report, a due-diligence deep dive, or chosen aspects only
Limits
public information only: no proprietary databases, interviews or real-time data, and it can't verify what it states

TLDR

From Claude Office Skills. You name the company (with its ticker if listed), its sector and region, whether it's public or private, the depth you need and what the research is for. The agent works through fixed frameworks: the revenue model (subscription, transaction, licensing, advertising, hardware or services), where the company sits from upstream to end user, Porter's five forces rated low, medium or high with a rationale, a positioning matrix of price against value, and SWOT. The report opens with a summary and a suitability verdict, then covers the business and its revenue streams, the market with TAM, SAM and SOM, competitors with market share, moats, management and board, insider and institutional ownership, margins, ROE and debt to equity, and risks rated by likelihood and impact with mitigations, ending with next steps and a disclaimer that it isn't investment advice. Its frontmatter lists document, spreadsheet and chart tools of the repo's office-mcp server, but its instructions never call them.

Company Research Skill

Overview

I help you conduct comprehensive company research for investment decisions, business development, or competitive intelligence. I analyze business models, competitive positioning, market dynamics, and management quality.

What I can do:

  • Business model canvas analysis
  • Competitive landscape mapping
  • SWOT and Porter's Five Forces analysis
  • Market size and growth assessment
  • Management and governance review
  • Risk identification

What I cannot do:

  • Access proprietary databases
  • Conduct primary research (interviews)
  • Provide real-time data
  • Verify information accuracy

How to Use Me

Step 1: Specify the Company

Tell me:

  • Company name and ticker (if public)
  • Industry/sector
  • Geography focus
  • Public or private
Step 2: Choose Research Scope
  • Quick Overview: 1-page summary
  • Standard Research: Comprehensive analysis
  • Deep Dive: Detailed due diligence
  • Specific Focus: Choose particular aspects
Step 3: Specify Use Case
  • Investment decision
  • Competitive intelligence
  • Partnership evaluation
  • M&A target assessment
  • Market entry analysis

Competitive Analysis Framework
Porter's Five Forces
  1. Threat of New Entrants: Barriers to entry, capital requirements
  2. Bargaining Power of Suppliers: Supplier concentration, switching costs
  3. Bargaining Power of Buyers: Customer concentration, price sensitivity
  4. Threat of Substitutes: Alternative solutions, switching costs
  5. Industry Rivalry: Number of competitors, market growth, differentiation

This research is based on publicly available information and AI analysis. It should not be considered as investment advice or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any securities.

  5. Indicate depth level needed (quick scan vs deep dive)
