hr-automation

Use it when
you're planning automations for the emails, reminders, approvals and checklists around hiring, onboarding, time off, reviews or departures
Auto-rejects applicants
its GPT-4 resume screen scores applicants out of 10; anyone under 5 is rejected and emailed automatically, with no person reviewing, while 5 to 7 are flagged for review
It does
maps each process to triggers and actions: candidate stage changes, interview invites and reminders, onboarding tasks from two weeks before day one, PTO routing, quarterly reviews, offboarding
You get
YAML outlines and checklists, such as an onboarding checklist with tasks for the new hire, manager and IT, escalating reminders and an n8n outline
Doesn't
connect to an HR system: the Greenhouse, Workday and BambooHR tools in its frontmatter are never called in its instructions

TLDR

From Claude Office Skills, based, it says, on n8n's HR workflow templates. You ask for an HR automation and the agent designs it from its templates. Recruiting covers a seven-stage pipeline from applied to hired, with confirmations, Slack alerts and stage-specific emails. Its AI screen gives GPT-4 the resume and job requirements and asks for skill and experience ratings from 1 to 10, without saying how they become the one score its rules act on: 8 or more advances the candidate, 5 to 7 is flagged for review, and under 5 is rejected and sent a rejection email automatically, with no person looking. Interview scheduling finds slots with a 15-minute buffer, sends invites and prep material, reminds everyone a day and an hour before and collects feedback. Onboarding runs from document collection and equipment two weeks out to a 30-day survey, with deadlines for the I-9, direct deposit and benefits. Time-off requests go to the manager for up to three days and to HR as well beyond that. Offboarding revokes email, SSO, VPN and building access at once on termination, removes licenses and repositories within 24 hours, and audits the revocations.

AI Resume Screening
ai_screening:
  model: gpt-4
  
  prompt: |
    Review this resume for the {job_title} position.
    
    Job Requirements:
    {job_requirements}
    
    Resume:
    {resume_text}
    
    Evaluate:
    1. Skills match (1-10)
    2. Experience match (1-10)
    3. Key strengths
    4. Potential concerns
    5. Recommended: Yes/No/Maybe
    
  automation:
    - if: score >= 8
      action: advance_to_screening
    - if: score 5-7
      action: flag_for_review
    - if: score < 5
      action: auto_reject + send_rejection
Interview Scheduling
