TLDR

It combines two other skills from the same bundle. grilling runs the interview: it works through your plan one open decision at a time, asking numbered questions, each with a recommended answer, until every decision is settled, and it looks up facts in your files itself instead of asking you. domain-modeling runs alongside: it calls out words that clash with GLOSSARY.md or are vague ("account": the customer or the user?), invents edge cases, checks your claims against the code, adds each settled term to GLOSSARY.md right away, and offers an ADR only for a decision that is hard to reverse, surprising, and a real trade-off. It's the usual first step of the bundle's workflow when you're working in a repo.
