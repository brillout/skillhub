hypit

Use it when
you want a video made: a remake of a reference video, a short drama, a creator-led social clip, or one from a brief
It does
studies a reference's frames alongside its speech, writes a brief and a creative treatment, directs image, video and voice models, then composes the video
You get
a finished video, watched and checked by the agent, plus the editable production in Hypit's Studio for timestamped comments
Keeps
its understanding in project files, the brief, treatment, analysis and timeline, so another session can pick the work up
Needs
the Hypit executable, plus HypiHub or your own model API keys for generation, which the skill doesn't include

Generation needs HypiHub or your own model API keys, which cost money; the skill agrees accounts and a budget with you before paid work.

TLDR

Hypit's own skill. The agent acts as director and producer, making creative choices and explaining them as the work develops. Hypit treats a video as one timeline and one canvas whose objects change over time; where speech leads, events are tied to words in the script rather than fixed times, so a new wording moves the picture with its meaning. A-roll is the performance carrying a passage; B-roll, captions, MG (motion graphics) and effects are composed around it, and new visual systems are written as project components in Hypit's own formats (SVML, SVS and SVRun). Before writing an image, video or voice prompt, the agent must read Hypit's direction guide for it, which records how the models respond. It brings you into choices about your goal, services and spending, and explains what is running during long generations. The SKILL.md ends with a map to dozens of reference files, from caption styling and sound mixing to downloading references with yt-dlp.
