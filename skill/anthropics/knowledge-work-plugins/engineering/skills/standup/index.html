TLDR

A standup command from the engineering plugin of Anthropic's knowledge-work plugins. It works two ways. Without connectors, you tell it what you did, such as "worked on the auth migration, reviewed 3 PRs, got blocked on the API rate limiting issue", and it structures that into a concise, action-oriented update. With connectors, /standup alone gathers the material: commits and PRs you opened, reviewed or merged, summarized at a high level, from source control; tickets moved to in progress or done, and upcoming sprint items, from a project tracker; relevant discussions and decisions from chat, with threads that need your reply flagged. The update has three sections, Yesterday, Today and Blockers, with ticket references where available and, for each blocker, its context and who can help. You can add nuance afterwards, or ask for it formatted for Slack, email or your team's standup tool.

How It Works

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        STANDUP                                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  STANDALONE (always works)                                       │
│  ✓ Tell me what you worked on and I'll structure it             │

│  ✓ Keep it concise and action-oriented                          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  SUPERCHARGED (when you connect your tools)                      │

│  + Project tracker: Ticket status changes                        │
│  + Chat: Relevant discussions and decisions                      │
│  + CI/CD: Build and deploy status                                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

What I Need From You

Option A: Let me pull it If your tools are connected, just say /standup and I'll gather everything automatically.

Option B: Tell me what you did "Worked on the auth migration, reviewed 3 PRs, got blocked on the API rate limiting issue."

Tips

  1. Run it every morning — Build a habit and never scramble for standup notes.
  2. Add context — After I generate, add any nuance about blockers or priorities.
  3. Share format — Ask me to format for Slack, email, or your team's standup tool.
