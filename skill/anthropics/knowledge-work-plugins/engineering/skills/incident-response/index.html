TLDR

A four-phase incident command from the engineering plugin of Anthropic's knowledge-work plugins. Triage rates severity, from SEV1 (service down for all users, an immediate all-hands response) to SEV4 (cosmetic, next business day), identifies the affected systems and users, and assigns an incident commander, communications and responders. Communicate drafts the internal status update and any customer message and sets up a war room and an update cadence. Mitigate records the steps taken and the timeline and confirms resolution. Postmortem writes a blameless document: summary, impact, a UTC timeline, root cause, five whys, what went well and poorly, action items with owner, priority and due date, and lessons learned. Status updates follow one template: severity, a status from investigating to resolved, impact, what's known, actions taken, next steps and a timeline. Updates stay factual and start before all the facts are in, and postmortems focus on systems and processes, not people. With monitoring, incident management or chat connected, it pulls alert details and metrics, creates or updates the incident, pages on-call and posts updates to the incident channel.

Manage an incident from detection through postmortem.

Usage

/incident-response $ARGUMENTS

Modes

/incident-response new [description]     # Start a new incident
/incident-response update [status]       # Post a status update
/incident-response postmortem            # Generate postmortem from incident data

If no mode is specified, ask what phase the incident is in.

Severity Classification

Level Criteria Response Time
SEV1 Service down, all users affected Immediate, all-hands
SEV2 Major feature degraded, many users affected Within 15 min
SEV3 Minor feature issue, some users affected Within 1 hour
SEV4 Cosmetic or low-impact issue Next business day

Communication Guidance

Provide clear, factual updates at regular cadence. Include: what's happening, who's affected, what we're doing, when the next update is.

Tips

  1. Start writing immediately — Don't wait for complete information. Update as you learn more.
  2. Keep updates factual — What we know, what we've done, what's next. No speculation.
  3. Postmortems are blameless — Focus on systems and processes, not individuals.
